# Atlas explorer

Mode: Operate

Audience: people investigating a recorded run. Job: find a place in the product and read what the run showed there. The index must accept a new spec kind as another row.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The explorer is a gazetteer. The index stays open and one plate shows the place you opened, its screen, and the events that leave it. The card grid and the detached evidence page are refused.

OWN-WORLD: Ink #1c2430 on ground #e7eef2. The header and the current row are #1d4e89. Failure is #b42318 and only marks a failed place, event, or moment. Type is Atkinson Hyperlegible. The plate is a white sheet. The index is a list with the status word on the right.

STORY: You pick a place or a spec in the index, read the plate, and follow an event or a moment. The index does not move. A recorded test is the same plate: the moment large, the steps beside it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A blue bar holds the product name, the run, and search. The left column lists places, then specs. The plate names the open place in large type, shows its screen, and lists the events that leave it. A failed event is red.

FORM: Gazetteer, first on the ordered list, seed key 2ba1f79c.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Signature interaction: choosing a place, a spec, or a recorded moment replaces only the plate.
